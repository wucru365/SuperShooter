Running the game:
In the Scenes folder open up the "chapter2" scene and press play, the game is a shooter game in which the mouse controls the orientation of the character, clicking shoots, and WASD moves the character. The enemies spawn on the platform and will now chase you and shoot you if you are within range. You have a health of 100, each of the enemy bullets do 5 damage, your bullets do 20 damage to enemies with 100 health. Killing all enemies will bring you to a win screen (scene with a sphere), dying to the enemies will bring you to a lost screen (scene with an oval). 

Assets and visual effects were added as part of HW4 and HW5. These include explosion effects when an enemy is killed, rain, as well as lighting updates and motion blur. The gameplay mechanics are still the same, however, there are more visual elements to the game now with a few particle systems (campfire, rain, explosion) that make the game look better. 

Sound effects and background music was added as part of HW6. There is a sound effect when the player shoots and when the enemies are destroyed. There is also background ambient music added as well. Another part of the game is adding in UI, there is a health bar and a score, the health bar goes down when enemies shoot you and the score increases by 10 for every enemy you destroy in the game. Pressing "m" will take you to a pause menu, where you can choose to quit the game (currently doesn't do anything other than printing "quitting"), and also choose to resume the game using your mouse. The game mechanics are still the same. As additional visual, I added animation to the character but only for running vs walking. If the character moves the character will move its arms accordingly.
